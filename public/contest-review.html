Reviewer progress

      

      

        Assign finalist reads
        
Separate from ordinary Yes / Maybe / No votes. First assign completed poems with 2+ initial Yes votes for a shortlist pass. Later, use the 1-Yes rescue pass. One reader per poem is the default; choose two or three independent readers for more opinions. Original voters are skipped for that poem. Committing new assignments emails the assigned readers.

        Act on filtered set: assign fourth reads
        
This acts on the submissions matching the filter above. One email gets every eligible fourth read; multiple emails split them roughly evenly. Preview shows the exact count and split without assigning anything. A queued fourth read becomes votable after the first three reviews are complete. Archived entries and entries already queued for a fourth read are skipped; a reviewer cannot be assigned an entry they have already reviewed. This does not email reviewers.
