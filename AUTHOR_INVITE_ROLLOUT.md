# Author invitation rollout — week of September 28, 2026

Goal: send a small, functional Poetry Please invitation pilot to authors this week, with a clear human support path. This is preparation, not authorization to send or to create invites for real authors.

## Existing path and limits

- Admin can generate an email-specific, expiring secure author invite and open a prefilled Gmail message; sending is manual. The link must go only to its intended recipient.
- The invite is intended to be claimed while signed in with the invited email. Do not promise that another address will work.
- Poetry Please already references `support@buttonpoetry.com` in password-reset help, but its monitoring and owner are not verified here.
- Do not describe invitation generation as automatic email delivery or claim that delivery is tracked.
- Do not add a new mail provider, auth scope, or deployment workflow for this week's pilot.

## Send gate

1. Name one staff owner for author replies and confirm that the chosen inbox is monitored this week. Use that address in the email and on the author help path; do not route replies to Sam by default.
2. With an approved internal test address, generate an invite, open the Gmail draft, inspect recipient/subject/body/unique URL and expiration, then send the test message manually.
3. Claim the invite as the matching address; confirm clear behavior for a mismatched address and an expired/already-used link. Verify the author can reach their own profile and associated-work/feedback area without admin controls.
4. Verify a reply or help request reaches the monitored inbox and that the staff owner can respond.
5. Review a first 2–3-author list for correct names, addresses, content associations, and consent/relationship context. Send the pilot manually; pause before a larger batch until those authors can claim and get help.

## Invitation copy (personalize before sending)

**Subject:** Your invitation to Poetry Please

Hi [first name],

We’re inviting you to Poetry Please, Button Poetry’s space for discovering and sharing poetry. We’ve gathered work associated with you and would love you to look it over, help us correct anything that’s off, and shape how your author page appears.

To get started, open your personal invitation: [unique secure invite link]

Please sign in with **[invited email address]**. This link is for that address and expires on **[date]**. If any work is missing or incorrectly attributed, you can tell us after signing in—we’ll review corrections before changing the public catalog.

Questions or trouble getting in? Reply to **[staff owner and email]** or email **[confirmed support inbox]**. You do not need to contact Sam directly.

Thanks,
[Button Poetry team / named staff sender]
