We’re inviting you to Poetry Please, Button Poetry’s space for discovering and sharing poetry. We’ve gathered work associated with you and would love you to look it over, help us correct anything that’s off, and shape how your author page appears.

To get started, open your personal invitation: [unique secure invite link]

Please sign in with **[invited email address]**. This link is for that address and expires on **[date]**. If any work is missing or incorrectly attributed, you can tell us after signing in—we’ll review corrections before changing the public catalog.

Questions or trouble getting in? Email **support@buttonpoetry.com** and our team will help.

Thanks,
[Button Poetry team / named staff sender]
